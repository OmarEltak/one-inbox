## STEP 0 — Fix Server Errors FIRST (do this before anything else)

12 pages including the homepage are flagged with **Server error (5xx)** because Cloudflare's Bot Fight Mode was blocking Googlebot. That is now fixed.

1. Go to Search Console → **Pages**
2. Click the row **"Server error (5xx)"**
3. Click **"Validate Fix"** at the top
4. Google will re-crawl within 1–3 days

Without this, Google keeps assuming your homepage is broken.

---

## How to Request Indexing

For each URL below:
1. Paste it into the **"Inspect any URL"** bar at the top of Search Console
2. Wait for the check (~5 seconds)
3. Click **"Request Indexing"**
4. Wait for confirmation
5. Move to next URL

Takes about 15 minutes per day for 10 URLs.

## Notes

**Discovered vs Crawled — quick reference:**
- **Discovered - not indexed** = Google knows the URL but hasn't crawled it yet. Just needs time. Request indexing helps.
- **Crawled - not indexed** = Google looked at it and decided not to index. Quality problem, not a crawl problem. Requesting indexing won't fix it — the page content needs improvement.

**Ignored on purpose:**
- Any `?lang=de`, `?lang=es`, or `?lang=ar` variants. English is the priority. The canonical + hreflang fix (deployed 2026-07-08) tells Google to consolidate to the clean English URL.
- The 4 "Crawled - not indexed" pages (`/blog`, `/privacy?lang=de`, etc.) — will resolve as Google re-crawls with the new canonical.

**After all 52 are submitted:**
Wait 7–14 days. Check Search Console → Pages again. The count in "Discovered" should drop as pages move to "Indexed". If any move to "Crawled - not indexed" instead, that page needs content improvement, not another indexing request.
